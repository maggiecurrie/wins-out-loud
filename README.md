# Wins Out Loud

**Your team's week, out loud.** A weekly 1:1 hub for managers and their teams.

🌐 **[Open the prototype](https://maggiecurrie.github.io/wins-out-loud/)**

A working prototype, open for anyone to use, adapt, and build on for their own team.

---

## The problem

- **Every 1:1 starts from memory.** Your manager asks how the week went, and Tuesday's big win has already vanished.
- **Review season is an archaeology dig** through months of email.
- **Great work goes unseen.** If you don't say it, nobody knows.

## How it works

1. **Talk it out.** Before your 1:1, tap the mic and talk through your week, messy and unscripted. (You can also type, or log wins as they happen.)
2. **Check the sort.** It sorts what you said into wins, in progress, and blockers. You decide what stays.
3. **Share with your manager.** One link opens a clean 1:1 page. Your manager ticks off what you covered and adds notes and next steps, then copies a recap.
4. **Show your receipts.** Every quarter, months of wins become a quarterly check-in, a self-review draft, resume bullets, or a post.

## Built for both sides

- **For you:** a running database of your work, with results, proof, and the stories behind it. Weekly 1:1s take minutes to prep, and quarterly check-ins and reviews come with receipts.
- **For your manager:** wins, work in progress, and blockers at a glance, without chasing anyone for updates, plus specific, dated examples for feedback and reviews.

## Privacy

- Your wins are saved only in your own browser. Nothing is stored on a server.
- A shared 1:1 page lives entirely inside its link, so only people you send it to can see it.
- Voice uses your browser's built-in speech recognition (Chrome, Edge, or Safari).
- If you use AI to polish an update, use a tool your company approves for work information.
